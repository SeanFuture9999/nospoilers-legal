The Services do not require account registration. You may voluntarily provide information by:

  • Contacting us by email for support, feedback, privacy inquiries, or data deletion requests

Gameplay progress and preferences—including levels, cultivation progress, inventory, skill collections, pet collections, settings, and other game-specific progress—are stored locally on your device. We do not operate an account server that retains a copy of this local game progress.

The Applications may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. The advertisement format depends on the Application:

Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID

Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking

6. Age Suitability

The Applications are intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us using the information below.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Because gameplay progress is stored locally on your device, it can be deleted directly by you:

  • In the Application: Use the reset progress or delete local data option where available. This action may be irreversible.
  • At the device level: Uninstalling an Application generally removes locally stored game data, subject to the operating system's backup and restore settings.
  • Information processed by Google services is retained according to Google's retention policies. You may also contact us to submit a privacy or deletion request.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled through the Services. The Applications do not require an account and primarily store gameplay progress locally, reducing the amount of player information retained by us.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the email address below.
